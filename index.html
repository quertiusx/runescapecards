How to Play

Goal

You both start with 30 Hitpoints. Bring your opponent to 0 to win.

Runes

Cards cost runes (the blue number). You get 1 rune on turn 1, 2 on turn 2... up to 10. They refill every turn. Whoever goes second gets a free Coins card.

Your turn

  1. You draw a card.
  2. Play cards: click a glowing card, then click where it goes (a spot on the table, or a target).
  3. Attack: click one of your glowing monsters, then click an enemy monster or your opponent.
  4. Press End Turn.

Monsters

Attack (bottom-left) is the damage it deals. Health (bottom-right) is how much it can take. When monsters fight, both hit each other. A monster can attack once per turn, starting the turn after you play it.

The Combat Triangle

⚔ Melee beats 🏹 Ranged beats ✦ Magic beats ⚔ Melee

A monster that beats its opponent's style deals DOUBLE damage, whether attacking or hitting back. Hover a target while attacking to see ×2 hints.

Keywords

  • Guard: must be attacked first.
  • Charge: can attack right away.
  • Venom: destroys any monster it damages.
  • Vampyric: its damage heals you.
  • Frozen: skips its next attack.

Controls: drag the scene or use arrow keys to look around the tavern · hover any card to read it · right-click or Esc to cancel · Enter to end your turn.

Card Codex

Both duellists use the same 43-card deck, shuffled.
